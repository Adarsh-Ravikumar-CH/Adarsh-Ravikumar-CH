Log 01 Nov 2026 Elderly Care & Rehabilitation

Medicine Buddy

Robotics Carnival · Leukerbad · EIII

The challenge: how can robots help older people live safely and independently at home? According to the WHO, only about half of people with a chronic illness take their medicines as prescribed. For someone living alone with poor eyesight, a missed dose can mean a hospital visit, and pill boxes are famously terrible at conversation.

So we built Medicine Buddy, a voice-first home assistant made of two connected robots:

  • A Reachy Mini you talk to. You set up your medicines by voice and it reminds you at the right time, even mid-conversation. It reads labels and letters aloud, alerts family if a dose is missed, and saves grandparents' stories and recipes for the grandchildren.
  • A Maker Arm with two cameras that finds the right pill from a text description and places it in your hand, so you never have to read a label yourself.

Tests passing, robots crashing, and a Wi-Fi network with other plans. We kept at it and had it all working when it mattered.

Team Tommaso Sommaruga, Orhun Efe Çiloğlu, Gaëtan Rukundo Bracke, Hamdi Kerem Işık and me. Hardware from NormaCore and Pollen Robotics.
