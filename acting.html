The season

From September 2025 to May 2026 I rehearsed and performed with PLUME, and ended up live on stage in front of 200 paying audience members.

  1. Lines

    Memorised and delivered down to the pauses, with the same precision every time.

  2. Rehearsals

    Months of work with the cast and the production team, on a schedule that doesn't move because opening night doesn't.

  3. Direction

    Notes from the director one evening, applied the next. Then again, until the scene works.

  4. Live

    When something goes off-script in front of an audience, you adjust on the spot and keep going.
